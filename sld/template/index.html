Understanding SLD

Specific Learning Difficulties

These difficulties are not related to intelligence — early support makes a difference.

Dyslexia

Affects reading, spelling, and language processing. Children may read slowly or confuse letters and words.

Dysgraphia

Affects writing skills, handwriting, and expressing thoughts on paper. Writing may feel tiring or frustrating.

Dyscalculia

Affects understanding numbers, calculations, time, and patterns. Children may struggle with basic math concepts.
