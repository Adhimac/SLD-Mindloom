It is not a diagnosis, but it may help you decide whether further evaluation or support could be beneficial. Answer honestly based on your child’s everyday learning experiences.

Instructions

Purpose of the Test

  • This test helps to observe your child’s natural learning abilities.
  • Honest and independent responses from the child are essential.
  • The results can help decide whether further evaluation or professional support may be helpful.

Parental Presence Is Required

  • A parent or guardian must be present while the child takes the test.
  • Your role is to observe, reassure, and support emotionally, not academically.
  • Stay nearby throughout the entire test session.

No Outside Help During the Test

  • Please do not help your child answer questions.
    • Avoid:
  • Explaining questions
  • Giving hints or clues
  • Correcting answers
  • Suggesting what to choose
The child’s responses should reflect their own understanding and ability.

When Support Is Allowed

You may assist your child only when necessary, such as:

  • Helping them stay calm or focused
  • Encouraging them to continue if they feel anxious or tired
  • Assisting with navigation (e.g., clicking “Next” if they are very young)

Create a Comfortable Environment

Ensure the test is taken in a quiet, distraction-free space. Make sure your child is:

  • Well-rested
  • Comfortable
  • Not rushed
A calm environment helps your child perform naturally.

Encourage, Don’t Pressure

Let your child know:

  • There are no right or wrong answers
  • This is not an exam
  • They can take their time
    • Positive encouragement helps reduce anxiety and improves cooperation.
