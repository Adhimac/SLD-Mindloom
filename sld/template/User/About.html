About Our SLD Diagnosis Platform

Empowering parents, educators, and doctors to better understand and manage Specific Learning Disabilities in children.

🧠 What is SLD?

Specific Learning Disability (SLD) refers to a group of learning disorders that affect a child’s ability to read, write, spell, or perform mathematical calculations. Early diagnosis and intervention are crucial to a child’s development.

💡 Our Mission

We aim to bridge the gap between diagnosis and treatment by offering an accessible, user-friendly platform for identifying and managing SLD through technology-driven solutions.

⚙️ Features

  • Secure doctor appointment booking system
  • Detailed reports and personalized recommendations

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Who We Help

  • Doctors and specialists managing learning disorders
  • Educators seeking early signs of SLD in students
